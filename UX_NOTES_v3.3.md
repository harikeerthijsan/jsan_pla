# v3.3 Industry UX

The reviewer workspace follows JSAN's public positioning around **Operate · Map · Intelligence · Engineer** and the delivery lifecycle **Mobilise · Collect · Operate · Process · Validate · Deliver**. The product focuses on the Validate stage: evidence, accuracy, compliance and human-reviewed QA.

## New reviewer controls

- Maximize/restore each of Plan, Longitudinal Profile, Cross Section and 3D Perspective.
- Minimize any view and restore it from the toolbar dock.
- Focus Workspace hides the pole/QC side panels without losing selection state.
- Reset Layout restores the four-view engineering arrangement.
- Keyboard shortcuts: `1` Plan, `2` Profile, `3` Cross Section, `4` 3D; `Esc` restores from maximized view.
- 3D point-cloud cleanup is compatible with the Potree 1.8 scene structure and no longer calls the missing `scene.removePointCloud()` method.

## Brand direction

Dark operational workspace, cyan engineering accent, restrained safety-status colours and compact evidence typography. This is an adaptation of the visual language on jsanconsulting.com, not a replacement for a formal corporate brand guide.
